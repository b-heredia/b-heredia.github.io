Short term

My next role

My immediate goal after i.c.stars is a paid technology apprenticeship, ideally starting in January 2027.

I’m especially interested in companies like IBM, Riot Games, GitLab and NVIDIA, where I can keep building my technical skills with real-world experience. I want a role that challenges me while I keep learning and growing in confidence.

Long term

Where I see myself in 3–5 years

I see myself established in the technology industry, with hands-on experience, stronger programming skills and the ability to build meaningful projects on my own. I want to keep growing in software development, artificial intelligence and application design.

Beyond working for an organization, I want to build my own company that uses technology to solve real problems and improve people’s lives. I want to create something meaningful, reach financial independence and become a founder and business owner.

Roles I’m pursuing

Cybersecurity and AI

I’m most interested in entry-level cybersecurity and AI roles, because I enjoy understanding how technology works and building tools with a real purpose, like TrustLens. I’m also open to roles where technology meets creativity or finance.

These roles fit what I already do well: troubleshooting, noticing patterns, documenting issues clearly and caring about the people a product serves.

02 — Preparation

Getting ready

Employer insights

What employers taught me

Conversations with Accenture, Equinix, Microsoft, Medline and others showed me how company cultures, expectations and career paths differ. They helped me think more intentionally about where I want to work and grow.

Advice I’m taking forward

Apply anyway

One piece of advice I’m keeping: keep learning, networking and applying, even when I don’t meet every requirement.

I shouldn’t disqualify myself because I don’t have every skill on a job description. I bring real skills, I’m open to learning, and I want to approach my career with confidence, curiosity and persistence.

03 — Brand

How I want to be known

Professional brand

Resourceful, dependable, solution-oriented

I want people to describe me as resourceful, dependable, analytical and solution-oriented: someone who doesn’t give up, takes initiative, communicates well and cares about the quality of her work.
