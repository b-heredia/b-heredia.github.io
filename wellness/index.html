The habits that keep me steady: financial, emotional, mental and physical.

Wellness

i.c.stars taught me that growth shouldn’t cost me my well-being. Here’s how I’m building habits to take care of myself.

01 — Financial

Stability before everything else

My goals

Short and long term

My central financial theme is stability before everything else.

Short term

  • Find steady employment after i.c.stars.
  • Rebuild my savings.
  • Pay down debt and improve my credit.

Long term

  • Become financially independent.
  • Invest in stocks.
  • Start my own business.

02 — Emotional

Quieting self-doubt

Imposter syndrome

Questioning whether I’m doing enough

One of my biggest emotional challenges has been self-doubt and uncertainty about my future. As i.c.stars comes to an end, I sometimes wonder whether I’m doing enough, or whether I’ll find the right opportunity.

I challenge that negative self-talk by remembering how far I’ve come, the skills I’ve built and the challenges I’ve already overcome. Instead of letting uncertainty make me doubt myself, I focus on what I can control and take one step at a time.

The affirmation I’m carrying forward:

“I am capable of learning, growing and creating opportunities for myself. I do not have to have everything figured out to move forward.”

03 — Mental

My toolbox for stress

Managing stress

Productivity shouldn’t cost my well-being

i.c.stars was mentally intense: deadlines, assignments, technical exercises and group projects. I learned that pushing nonstop without breaks becomes overwhelming.

What works for me:

  • Break big assignments into small, manageable tasks.
  • Focus on one priority at a time.
  • Ask questions when I need clarity.
  • Notice when I need to step back and recharge.
  • Set healthy boundaries when responsibilities pile up.

I also learned that asking for help doesn’t mean I’m incapable. It’s part of learning and becoming a better problem solver.

04 — Physical

Making movement part of my life

My routine

Resistance bands at home

I’ve become more aware of how much my physical well-being matters while I pursue my goals. I’m building a home workout routine with resistance bands, so exercise becomes part of my lifestyle and not just something I do when I have extra time.

Along with movement, I’m focusing on drinking enough water and getting consistent sleep. Taking care of my body is an investment in staying focused, productive and energized.
