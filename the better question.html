How it works

No magic. Just a better question.

No AI runs on this page

The Better Question structures your thinking and writes a prompt. The answering happens in the AI tool you choose.

The meter uses simple rules

It checks whether each of the five parts is filled in with enough detail. It can't judge whether your question is "right", only whether it's complete.

Nothing is stored or sent

Everything you type stays in your browser and disappears when you close the page.
