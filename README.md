## About Me

Machines are built to predict. I'm interested in what they can't. I design and build with artificial intelligence, treating it as a creative material to question, shape and push past the obvious.

I don't treat AI as a shortcut. I see it as a collaboration: fast, tireless, and dependent on the questions it's given. My work is about asking better ones.

I'm currently in the [i.c.stars](https://www.icstars.org/) internship program in Chicago, building technical foundations alongside business and leadership skills. I'm open to conversations about roles and collaborations.
