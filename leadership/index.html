Showing up, stepping up and leading at i.c.stars and before it.

Leadership

At i.c.stars I learned that leadership isn’t a title. It shows up in kindness, initiative, creativity and noticing what people need.

01 — i.c.stars

Moments that shaped me

Team Week

Leading through small acts of kindness

During Team Week, I noticed two people didn’t have money for lunch. I shared my food and made plates for them.

It reminded me that leadership starts with noticing what others need and acting when you can help. Sometimes the smallest action is what makes someone feel supported and included.

Gifts Project

A tree made of wire

For my Gifts Project, I built a tree out of hardware wire. I had to bend and shape each wire carefully so it wouldn’t break.

That became a lesson for me. The same way I handled the wire with care so the tree could take shape, I need to be intentional about taking care of myself so I can grow.

I found that one of my gifts is seeing meaning in everyday experiences and connecting creativity with personal growth.

Community Role

UX Researcher

My community role was UX Researcher. While watching people go through the tea experience, I noticed some of them weren’t sure how it worked or what to do next.

So I created tea notes to make the experience easier to understand. It taught me to observe people’s needs before designing a solution, and it confirmed how much I enjoy making things clearer and more accessible.

Tea Host

Learning from Lilah Jones

As a Tea Host, my guest was Lilah Jones, a coach who shared her nontraditional career path and her return from the technology industry.

She showed me that careers don’t always follow a straight line. You can bring past experience into a new industry and build your own path. I don’t have to follow one set path to be successful.

Dream Speech

Becoming a humanitarian business leader

In my Dream Speech, I described my goal of becoming a business leader.

My long-term vision is to build a company that meets humanitarian needs, using business and technology to create real solutions. I want it to be financially sustainable and to make a positive difference in people’s lives.

That vision is the kind of leader I want to be: one who combines entrepreneurship, innovation, compassion and purpose.

Teamwork

What I learned about leading together

Working with my teammates made me more comfortable sharing ideas, contributing to solutions and taking responsibility for finishing project work.

I learned that good teamwork needs everyone to communicate and own their part. I also learned to set boundaries, ask for support, and expect accountability to be shared.

What I learned about leadership

Leadership isn’t limited to managing people or holding authority. It can look like kindness, initiative, creativity, listening and noticing a chance to help. I want to keep growing into a leader who understands people’s needs and uses her abilities to create meaningful change.
